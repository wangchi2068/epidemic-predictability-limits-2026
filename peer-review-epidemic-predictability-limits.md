# Peer review: 《传染病预测视界的机制基准与实证校准差距》 (Mechanistic Benchmarks for Epidemic Forecast Horizons and the Empirical Calibration Gap)

**Material reviewed.** `uploads/main.pdf` (35 pages, XeLaTeX/`ctex`, double-blind anonymised, no author or affiliation block) and `uploads/main.tex` (1,012 lines), together with the companion public repository https://github.com/wangchi2068/epidemic-predictability-limits-2026, whose `main.pdf` and `main.tex` are byte-identical to the submitted attachments. Reference verification used Crossref, Europe PMC and publisher records. Page numbers below refer to the rendered PDF; line numbers refer to `main.tex`.

---

## 1. Summary

The manuscript asks how far ahead a mechanistic epidemic model can forecast before its own irreducible variance exceeds a stated error tolerance, and how that theoretical limit compares with the lead time forecasting systems actually achieve. Section 3 develops the theory. Theorem 1 establishes the sharp conditional-variance floor under squared loss; Lemmas 1–3 and Theorems 2–3 decompose relative mean squared error into an intrinsic process term and a lognormal parameter-extrapolation term, and prove strict monotonicity in the forecast horizon; Theorem 4 derives the Fisher information and Cramér–Rao bound for a negative-binomial likelihood; Theorem 5 gives an exact finite-horizon variance closed form for a fixed-dispersion NB2 macroscopic renewal model, with a geometric divergence term `[(1+1/k_agg)^h − 1]`. Section 4 verifies these results by Monte Carlo over a parameter grid.

Sections 5 and 6 supply a four-layer empirical architecture. Contact-tracing cohorts from Hong Kong and Guinea (Section 5.2, Table 2, Figure 3) establish strong offspring overdispersion. A panel of up to 51 US state jurisdictions across seven respiratory epidemic phases (Section 5.3, Table 3) yields plug-in NB2 mechanistic horizons with state-level medians of 2.2–7.1 weeks; paired against a persistence baseline over 294 state-phase units, the median ratio of mechanistic to realised horizon is 1.79. A descriptive error ledger (Table 5) attributes a median 55.0% of observed error to a model–observation algebraic residual. Section 6 audits three locked release versions of the CDC FluSight Forecast Hub, finding that a plug-in mechanistic predictor, even when run on final-vintage data, attains only 0.405–0.554 empirical coverage for nominal 95% intervals, with pre-peak coverage falling to 0.140–0.385.

The concluding claim is that the mechanistic horizon functions as a statistical outer envelope for realised operational lead time, not as a model-free physical limit.

## 2. Strengths

1. **Theorem 5 and Lemma 4 survive independent recomputation.** Iterating the first-order inhomogeneous recursion of Eq. (31) numerically and comparing against the printed closed form over 2,688 grid points (R ∈ {0.8,…,3.0} × k ∈ {0.1,…,1000} × I₀ ∈ {10,…,1000} × h ∈ {1,…,12}) gives a maximum relative error of 1.5×10⁻¹³. The four boundary limits of Lemma 4 (q→R, R=1, k_agg→∞, I₀→∞) agree with the continuous extension to within 10⁻⁹. The explicit q→R switch to `I₀hR^h + I₀²R^h(1−R^h)` to avoid 0/0 floating-point cancellation is the kind of detail only a working numerical implementation produces.

2. **Table 4 (Section 5.3, p. 20) is a deliberately constructed self-falsification.** On the same panel, window, parameter set and τ, only the macroscopic variance structure is swapped across NB2 / NB1 / Poisson / microscopic GW columns. Recomputation confirms that substituting Poisson lengthens the phase-wise median horizon by a factor of 1.56–2.56 (mean 1.96). The calibration of ϕ (setting the one-step conditional variance equal to NB2 at μ=I₀, giving `ϕ=1+RI₀/k_agg`) correctly isolates structure from scale. Section 7.3 then states outright that macroscopic variance structure is the primary source of uncertainty in the headline number — a concession most manuscripts would bury in supplementary material.

3. **The admission-threshold selection bias is quantified and then refused as supporting evidence** (Section 5.1, p. 16; Table 7, p. 23). The authors write: 由于该偏倚方向与本文“机制视界高于实测时效”这一结论的方向一致，本文不将其视为支持性证据，而按保守口径处理 ("because the direction of this bias coincides with the direction of our conclusion, we do not treat it as supporting evidence"). Table 7 rescans thresholds at ≥30/≥20/≥10 cases; recomputation reproduces the reported RSV declines of −29.4% and −55.5% exactly.

4. **The paired state-level analysis retracts a stronger reading the aggregate would have permitted** (Section 5.5, p. 22). Rather than resting on the phase-level median ratio of 1.11–4.00, the authors re-pair 294 units within jurisdiction, report a median of 1.79 with IQR [1.10, 3.36], and disclose that 21.1% of units have ratios below 1. An exact sign test at p < 10⁻¹⁵ is voluntarily downgraded on spatial-correlation grounds.

5. **The external audit reaches an auditable standard of version locking** (Section 6.1, pp. 24–25). Seasons 2023–24 and 2024–25 are pinned to upstream commit hashes `dde0d620…` and `1b5b6153…`, both verified character-for-character against the repository; the 2025–26 season, which has no annotated tag upstream, is pinned instead by a 57-file SHA-256 manifest, and the downgrade is stated rather than glossed. The strict four-way intersection gives all three models in Table 9 identical n (1,337/1,315/1,376), so the paired differences are directly interpretable.

6. **Section 6.6 (p. 29) keeps the two error differentials dimensionally distinct and concedes that one can be negative**: Gap^SE_h 是经验均方误差与模型方差之间的代数差额，在有限样本或模型设定偏误下并不保证非负 ("is an algebraic residual … not guaranteed non-negative under finite samples or model misspecification"). Table 5 duly contains a negative entry. Mislabelling an absorbing identity such as `RelMSE_obs = CV² + P_quad + E_res` as a variance decomposition is the standard failure mode in this literature, and the manuscript avoids it.

7. **Section 4.2 reports the numerical checks that failed.** Corollary 2 attains only 5/12 first-order agreement under the negative-binomial setting and is explicitly downgraded to a directional claim; the renewal implementation of Proposition 3 is reported at an empirical/theoretical ratio of 0.27–1.02. Printing a ratio of 0.27 is materially different from reporting only the grid points that agreed.

## 3. Weaknesses

Ranked by how far each propagates into the manuscript's conclusions.

**W1 — The citation layer fails in three load-bearing places, and one of them anchors the novelty of the central theorem.** Reference [20] does not exist: its DOI returns HTTP 404, its volume-article slot belongs to an unrelated photonics paper, its title duplicates reference [24], and the stated author group never wrote it. That reference is the sole citation supporting the sentence that positions Theorem 5 as an increment on prior work. Reference [2] is cited for the opposite of its published conclusion. Reference [4] is cited as the data source for three seasons it does not cover. This matters because the mathematics is sound but its *placement in the literature* is not currently checkable, and Section 2 is where a reader establishes what is new.

**W2 — Two load-bearing numerical statements outside the main tables are wrong, and one of them is self-contradictory on its face.** The cross-structure ratio “1.31--2.29 倍” (Section 5.3, L657) is not reproducible from any column or combination of Table 4. The state-level scaling figure “3.5--8.4 周” in Section 3.6 (L428) is quoted alongside a CV²_∞ range whose upper half makes the radicand of Eq. (27) negative, so the equation has no real solution there. Both sentences are relied on elsewhere — the first by Section 7.3 and the abstract's framing, the second by the reader's sense of when Eq. (27) applies.

**W3 — The health-equity scaling law in Section 7.2 requires a regime the manuscript's own measurements exclude, and a policy recommendation is issued from it anyway.** `h*_low/h*₀ ≈ √(ρ_low/ρ₀)` holds when the horizon is parameter-identification-limited. Figure 4(b) and Section 5.3 report CV²_macro(h*)/τ² between 0.73 and 0.89 across all seven phases, i.e. process variance consumes most of the error budget. The conditional clause is present in the text but the conflict is never disclosed.

**W4 — None of the 36 coverage rates in Tables 9 and 10 carries any uncertainty measure, while the WIS comparisons in the same sections do.** The most-repeated single value, the pre-peak Hub coverage of 0.419, rests on n = 43 state-weeks that the manuscript elsewhere argues are not independent. The same value is restated in the abstract, in Section 1.2, in Section 6.4 and in Section 8 without n.

**W5 — Section 8 restates a hedged finding without its hedge.** The conclusion asserts that the mechanistic component 构成了实测误差的刚性基底 ("constitutes a rigid floor of observed error") unconditionally, while Table 5 contains a phase where the model variance is 114.1% of observed error. The body of the paper reserves this exit; the conclusion and abstract do not use it.

**W6 — The only figure supporting the microscopic layer is internally contradictory.** The Figure 3 caption and the in-figure legend assign the plotted elements to opposite models, and the caption names an element that does not appear in the figure; in the third panel the two series are the same hue.

## 4. Detailed comments

### Major

**1. Reference [20] (`dilauro2023intrinsic`) points to a publication that does not exist, and it anchors the novelty claim for Theorem 5.** Section 2.1, p. 2; `main.tex` L161; entry printed on p. 34.

Original: 近期，Di Lauro 等[20] 在一般连续时间分支更新框架下证明，疫情系统的内在随机性方差本身满足随时间演化的递推更新方程，且条件方差随均值平方增长……本文在此脉络上深入，聚焦于离散周度尺度的固定离散度 NB2 宏观更新模型，推导了精确的有限步方差闭式解 ("Di Lauro et al. [20] recently proved … the present work extends this line").

Four independent checks: (i) `10.1038/s42005-023-01233-3` returns HTTP 404 from the Crossref REST API — the only 404 among the 42 DOIs in the bibliography, all others returning 200; (ii) *Communications Physics* 6, article 117 (2023) is `10.1038/s42005-023-01225-w`, Liu/Menyuk/Chembo on microwave phase noise in Kerr frequency combs; (iii) the title printed for [20] resolves to `10.1038/s42005-023-01265-2`, *Commun. Phys.* 6:146 (2023), which is the manuscript's own reference [24] — the same title therefore appears twice in one bibliography under two numbers and is cited in two different literature strands; (iv) the stated author group's actual joint paper is *Dynamic survival analysis for non-Markovian epidemic models*, *J. R. Soc. Interface* 19(191):20220124 (2022). The sentence immediately following the citation is what positions Theorem 5 relative to prior work, so with the anchor removed the increment cannot be assessed. **Suggestion:** determine which paper was intended. If Penn et al., delete [20], merge into [24], and rewrite the sentence to match what Penn et al. actually establish (an aleatoric/epistemic variance decomposition for general time-varying branching processes, which does not match the wording 连续时间分支更新框架下的递推更新方程). If the Dynamic Survival Analysis work was intended, correct title, journal, year, volume/pages and DOI, and confirm that it supports the proposition. Either way, rewrite the novelty statement jointly with comment 32.

**2. The cross-structure ratio “1.31--2.29 倍” cannot be reproduced from Table 4, and both endpoints are wrong.** Section 5.3, p. 20; L657.

Original: 在结构上更接近 Poisson 或 NB1 的宏观更新模型下，同一面板给出的视界约为本文报告值的 1.3--2.3 倍（逐阶段 1.31--2.29 倍） ("under macroscopic renewal models structurally closer to Poisson or NB1, the same panel yields horizons roughly 1.3–2.3 times our reported values").

Recomputing all seven rows of Table 4 gives: Poisson/NB2 1.56–2.56; NB1/NB2 1.06–1.53; microscopic GW/NB2 1.56–2.55; NB1 and Poisson pooled 1.06–2.56; column mean/NB2 1.48–2.21; geometric mean/NB2 1.40–1.98. No construction, including min, max, arithmetic and geometric means, reproduces 1.31–2.29. The printed lower bound 1.31 is the single NB1/NB2 cell for the Delta row (1.312); the printed upper bound 2.29 corresponds to no cell at all (the closest is GW/NB2 for influenza 2022–23 at 2.282). Under either narrow reading the printed interval is *narrower* than the truth, so the error understates evidence that runs against the paper's headline number — the true Poisson upper bound is 2.56. **Suggestion:** state the comparator explicitly and write, for example, 换用 Poisson 结构后逐阶段为 1.56–2.56 倍（均值 1.96），换用 NB1 为 1.06–1.53 倍, and propagate the correction to item 5 of the Section 7.3 limitations list.

**3. The state-level scaling values in Section 3.6 are incompatible with the CV²_∞ range quoted in the same sentence: the radicand is negative.** Section 3.6, p. 11; L428.

Original: 但在表 3 列报的州级实际工作点（I₀ 中位数 37--757，对应 CV²_∞ = 0.03--0.70）上，相同的 C 仅给出 3.5--8.4 周的理论标度值；当发病规模过低导致 CV²_∞ ≥ τ²（I₀ ≲ 10）时，根号内部转为负值 ("at the state-level operating points … the same C yields only 3.5–8.4 weeks; when CV²_∞ ≥ τ², i.e. I₀ ≲ 10, the radicand turns negative").

Eq. (27) is `h*_ref(C) ≈ (μ_g/7)·√[C(τ² − CV²_∞)/(1/R+1/k)]`. At the paper's primary τ = 0.5, the radicand is negative whenever CV²_∞ > 0.25. The sentence's own upper bound is 0.70. The sign of the radicand depends only on (τ² − CV²_∞) and not on R or k: exhausting (R,k) over {(1.1,1), (1.271,56.6), (1.302,23.7), (1.554,11.2), (1.1,0.434), (1.1,0.111)} at CV²_∞ = 0.70 gives no solution in any case. Back-solving, 3.5 weeks corresponds to CV²_∞ ≈ 0.21, i.e. I₀ ≈ 100. The stated failure threshold is also wrong: with the sentence's own R = 1.1, k = 1, `CV²_∞ = (1+R/k)/[I₀(R−1)] = 2.1/(0.1·I₀)` equals τ² = 0.25 at I₀ = 84, not 10. This is consequential rather than typographical: three of the seven phases in Table 3 have state-level median I₀ below 84 — influenza 2022–23 (I₀ = 55), RSV 2024–25 (76) and RSV 2025–26 (38) — so Eq. (27) is inapplicable at the median operating point of nearly half the epidemic phases, whereas the current text implies failure only in extreme small-count settings. **Suggestion:** recompute and correct all three numbers, and add an explicit applicability condition: 式 (27) 要求 CV²_∞ < τ²，在 τ=0.5 口径下即 I₀ ≳ 84（R=1.1，k=1），表 3 中流感 2022–23 与两季 RSV 的中位工作点不满足该条件.

**4. The equity scaling law of Section 7.2 holds under a condition the paper's own measurements contradict, and a resource-allocation recommendation follows from it without qualification.** Section 7.2, p. 30; L884.

Original: 在两地区其他动力学参数、初始规模和过程方差近似相同，且视界主要受参数辨识误差约束的领先阶条件下……理论机制视界近似满足相对标度关系：h*_low/h*₀ ≈ √(ρ_low/ρ₀) ("under leading-order conditions in which the horizon is principally constrained by parameter-identification error …").

The scaling requires process variance to be negligible relative to τ². Section 5.3 and Figure 4(b) report CV²_macro(h*)/τ² with cross-state medians of 0.73–0.89 in all seven phases, leaving 11%–27% of the error budget to the parameter term. Halving ρ also halves the *observed* I₀, and CV²_∞ ∝ 1/I₀, so process variance doubles and cannot be held approximately fixed. Working through the consequence: at a baseline CV²/τ² of 0.10 the ratio is 0.667 against the claimed 0.707, and the law is a reasonable approximation; at 0.73 and 0.89 — the manuscript's own measured range — halving ρ pushes CV² across τ² and the horizon does not shrink by a factor of 0.707 but ceases to exist. The conditional clause and the disclaimer 该式为条件性标度关系而非完整因果分解 are present, but the conflict with Section 5.3 is never stated, and the sentence proceeds directly to 提示资源配置部门应向监测薄弱辖区倾斜常态哨点与抽样资源 — and surveillance-sparse jurisdictions are precisely the small-I₀, large-CV² case that least satisfies the condition. **Suggestion:** either (a) state that the relation holds only where CV²_macro(h*) ≪ τ², cite the 0.73–0.89 range from Figure 4(b) to show that the empirical operating points lie outside that region, and demote the policy statement to a qualitative remark for parameter-limited surveillance settings; or (b) retain the conclusion but derive the joint scaling in which CV² and C both vary with ρ. Option (b) will generally give a steeper decay than √ρ, which strengthens the authors' point, but it must be derived rather than asserted.

**5. All 36 coverage rates in Tables 9 and 10 are reported without uncertainty, and the most-cited value rests on n = 43 non-independent units.** Section 6.3 Table 9 (p. 26), Section 6.4 Table 10 (p. 28); restated in the abstract (L92), Section 1.2, Section 6.4 and Section 8.

Original (abstract): 各模型在峰前期普遍面临经验覆盖率骤降（Hub 集成 95% 覆盖率降至 0.419--0.852，机制预测器仅 0.140--0.385；峰后期集成恢复至 0.925--0.964）.

The manuscript applies a 10-region HHS cluster bootstrap to the WIS comparisons (Section 6.1, L774) but nothing at all to the coverage rates, so two headline metrics receive different standards of uncertainty treatment. Independence-assuming Jeffreys intervals — themselves a lower bound on the true width — give: v1.0.0 pre-peak, n = 108, 0.852, [0.776, 0.909]; v1.1.0 pre-peak, n = 343, 0.589, [0.536, 0.640]; v1.2.0 pre-peak, n = 43, 0.419, [0.280, 0.567]. The three pre-peak strata differ in n by a factor of eight, yet the abstract merges them into the single range 0.419–0.852 without reporting n. The 43 units are state-weeks driven by a common variant, a common season and a single NHSN reporting regime; the manuscript argues exactly this in Section 6.1 and in item 10 of Section 7.3, and acts on it for WIS. The table note for Table 10 does address the post hoc nature of the stratification and warns against single-cause attribution, but neither addresses sampling uncertainty. **Suggestion:** (a) add cluster-bootstrap 95% intervals by HHS region to every coverage cell in Tables 9 and 10, matching the WIS treatment; (b) report n alongside the pre-peak values wherever they appear in the abstract and Section 1.2; (c) if the v1.2.0 interval proves too wide to support 骤降, describe that season as directionally consistent with the prior two but sample-limited rather than folding it into a single range.

**6. The conclusion describes the mechanistic component as a rigid floor without the qualification the body supplies, and one phase violates it.** Section 8, p. 31; L912.

Original: (3) 误差记账表明显式机制分量构成了实测误差的刚性基底，模型—观测代数差额中位占比达 55.0%，吸收了现实中的非平稳均值漂移与报告延迟扰动 ("the explicit mechanistic component constitutes a rigid floor of observed error").

A floor requires the mechanistic component to lie at or below observed error. The JN.1 h = 1 row of Table 5 does not satisfy it: median CV²_macro = 359.13 against median RelMSE_obs = 332.34, so CV² alone exceeds total observed error by 8.1%, reaching 114.1% once P_quad = 19.97 is added. The residual share for that row is printed as −0.0% and the share-positive figure is 49%, meaning that in more than half the states in that configuration the mechanistic variance is a ceiling rather than a floor. Across all 21 rows the JN.1 shares (−0.0% / 18.6% / 31.1%) and the RSV 2025–26 shares (15.2 / 10.8 / 13.1%) are the outliers, the latter also being the only phase non-monotone in h. The body does reserve an exit — Section 6.6 concedes non-negativity is not guaranteed, Section 5.4 restricts the identity to descriptive accounting, and the Table 1 row for the state-level layer says 部分短步长或低残差配置中机制分量可接近实测误差 — but Table 1 says *approaches* where the data say *exceeds by 14%*, and Section 8 drops every qualification. **Suggestion:** rewrite Section 8 as 在多数阶段—步长配置中构成实测误差的主要基底（21 个配置中 20 个跨州中位差额为正），但在 JN.1 阶段机制方差与实测误差相当甚至超出, and correct the Table 1 wording from 可接近实测误差 to 可达到或超过实测误差.

**7. Reference [2] (Scarpino & Petri) is cited for the reverse of its published conclusion, in the opening paragraph that sets up the paper's motivation.** Section 1.1, p. 1; L106.

Original: Scarpino 与 Petri[2] 从信息论角度刻画了突发疫情在流行阈值附近的排列熵突变，指出系统在临界状态下由于涨落发散而导致预测难度阶跃 ("… showing that divergent fluctuations at criticality produce a step change in forecasting difficulty").

*Nature Communications* 10:898 (2019) does use permutation entropy as a model-free predictability measure and does identify a fundamental entropy barrier for disease time-series forecasting, so the first clause is defensible. Its conclusion, however, is that this barrier is often beyond the time scale of single outbreaks, implying prediction is likely to succeed, and it attributes cross-disease differences in predictability to model drift and social-network heterogeneity rather than to critical fluctuations. The manuscript joins the two clauses with a causal 因而 that reverses the direction of the source. **Evidence strength:** abstract-level. The publisher record and the PMC record agree, but I did not obtain the full text; the editor should ask the authors to verify against the full paper. **Suggestion:** restate accurately and use the contrast constructively — for example, note that the entropy barrier typically exceeds single-outbreak time scales, then distinguish the present work as addressing a different limit (an estimation-precision lower bound conditional on a working model and inference rule). The two-notion distinction already made at the end of Section 2.1 could be moved forward into the introduction to do this work.

**8. The vintage protocol controls temporal causality but deliberately inverts version causality, and the resulting bias is asserted rather than quantified — unlike the threshold bias, which is fully quantified.** Section 6.2 step 1, p. 25, L780; evaluation-positioning paragraph L772.

Original: 提取截止于该原点前 7 天（即预测基准日前 7 天）的最终版周度发病序列……需要说明，输入序列按原点因果截断，采用最终修订版以隔离初报噪音 ("… truncated causally at the origin, using the final revised version to isolate first-report noise").

Truncating at the origin correctly prevents post-origin data from entering. But at origin t the operational system saw the first-report value for t, whereas the mechanistic predictor sees a revision finalised months later. The manuscript is explicit about this asymmetry in four places (L772, L713, Section 7.3 item 2, and the external-audit row of Table 1), and the coverage conclusion survives it in the right direction: final-vintage data favour the mechanistic predictor, so severe under-coverage under favourable conditions is a stronger finding, not a weaker one. Two consequences are nevertheless unhandled. (a) The WIS comparison runs the other way. Table 9 ranks the mechanistic predictor alongside the Hub ensemble and official baseline (59.94 / 170.88 / 116.72 against 34.00 / 85.27 / 60.24) and Section 6.3 concludes that the ensemble's WIS is below both. Part of the ensemble's apparent disadvantage is the first-report noise it carries, so that gap is not a pure skill difference; the qualification stated at L772 is not repeated where Table 9 is interpreted. (b) `k_agg` and R are also estimated from final-vintage series, whose variance is smaller than that of first-report series, which biases CV²_macro downward and h* upward. The same holds for the state-level panel (Section 5.1, frozen final-report snapshots). The manuscript acknowledges that 面临初报延迟时有效时效预期更短 but does not quantify the magnitude, while the admission-threshold bias — a bias in the same direction — receives a full threshold sweep. **Suggestion:** (a) add one sentence where Table 9 is interpreted, stating that the WIS difference is not readable as pure modelling skill and that only the coverage conclusion is unaffected by the asymmetry; (b) run a bounded additional check using the contemporaneously available vintage truth in the FluSight archive (`data/flusight/*/target-hospital-admissions.csv` supports this) to re-estimate R and k_agg for one season and report the change in h*. If the upstream vintage cannot be reconstructed — the repository `MANIFEST.md` states that the panel processing chain cannot be replayed offline — say so explicitly, so that the two same-direction biases are held to the same standard.

### Minor

**11. The abstract's 最大偏差 7.6% has no counterpart anywhere in the body.** Abstract, p. 1; L92 is the only occurrence of `7.6` in the manuscript. (32)–(34) term by term. The editor should ask the authors to make that comparison and report it.

These study intrinsic limits to outbreak forecasting precision with the coefficient of variation as the central quantity — the same dimensional apparatus as the present CV², RelMSE and relative-variance divergence. The manuscript cites O'Regan & Drake [26] but not these.
- The meteorological predictability lineage. Defining the horizon as the first crossing of an error tolerance comes from numerical weather prediction, and the bibliography contains no meteorological source. Offered as a prompt rather than a requirement; I did not run a systematic search on this point.

**Suggestion:** add Held et al. to the first strand of Section 2.1 and delimit the increment explicitly; add Drake in Section 2.2 or Section 1.1. ### Reproducibility

The companion repository was audited read-only over the public HTTP route on 2026-09-19. No code was executed and no repository value was compared against the PDF, so what follows establishes that the artefacts exist and are internally labelled, not that the numbers agree.

**28. What the repository provides.** It is public, MIT-licensed, approximately 71 MB, with 22 commits. `scripts_v2/` holds 37 Python files including an orchestrator `make_all.py` and a consistency gate `check_consistency.py` (34,651 B). All six body figures exist at the paths referenced by `\includegraphics`, and `tables_v3/` contains 18 machine-generated `.tex` fragments covering all nine `\input` targets. `MANIFEST.md` documents the source-JSON → script → table-fragment chain for each table and correctly identifies Tables 1 and 11 as hand-written — which matches the source. Intermediate results are committed, not only finished figures: `flusight_v1.0/1.1/1.2_extended.csv`, `state_phases.json`, `state_rolling.json`, `region_cluster_bootstrap.json`, `horizon_robustness.json` and `sensitivity_v4.json`, plus two self-critical notes (`hub_pooled_metric_NOTE.md`, `predictor_sensitivity_NOTE.md`). Raw data are committed rather than merely cited: all three FluSight seasons with a 204-file SHA-256 manifest, the state hospitalisation panel, and both contact-tracing cohorts. The two upstream commit hashes printed in Section 6.1 match the repository character for character, and v1.2.0 indeed has no upstream commit SHA, so the manuscript's explanation on that point is accurate.

**29. What it does not provide.** `environment.yml` is the only dependency file and pins version ranges rather than exact versions; there is no lock file, no `Dockerfile`, no CI configuration and no `Makefile`. The file's own comment concedes that the reported integer resampling counts depend on RNG generator behaviour and may change across major versions. I did not read the scripts, so I cannot confirm whether seeds are fixed in code. Separately, `MANIFEST.md` states that the raw-to-frozen processing chain for the state panel cannot be replayed offline because the upstream historical target-data vintages cannot be reconstructed — a real reproducibility limit, disclosed by the authors rather than concealed, and directly relevant to comment 8. **Suggestion:** add a lock file and a minimal CI job that runs `check_consistency.py`; state the seed policy in the manuscript.

**30. `environment.yml` lists `lightgbm` and `torch`, neither of which has any counterpart in the described method chain** (NB2 renewal model, maximum likelihood, bootstrap). Section 1.1 mentions machine learning only as the third modelling paradigm in the literature. This is either environment cruft or an undisclosed analysis branch. **Suggestion:** remove them or disclose what they were used for.

**31. Three smaller repository/manuscript mismatches.** `MANIFEST.md` records no generating script for Figure 1 although `scripts_v2/make_framework_figure.py` exists, while all five other figures are mapped. The README claims supplementary tables S1–S9 but `tables_v3/` contains only `supp_s1`, `supp_s2`, `supp_s5` and `supp_s8`; S3/S4/S6/S7/S9 may be embedded in `supplementary/supplementary_tables.tex`, which I did not read — this matters because several body values exist only in those tables (see comment 34). ## 5. Overall recommendation

**Major revision. Score: 5 / 10.**

The theoretical core is real and it holds up: Theorem 5 and Lemma 4 reproduce to floating-point precision over 2,688 grid points, and 20 of 25 derived quantities I recomputed match the printed values exactly. The methodological self-discipline — Table 1's evidence-boundary matrix, Table 4's structural sensitivity, the threshold sweep in Table 7, the paired analysis in Section 5.5, the reporting of failed numerical checks in Section 4.2 — is better than most submissions in this area. The score is held at 5 because the failures are concentrated in exactly the places a reader relies on to situate and to trust the work: a citation that does not exist anchoring the novelty of the central theorem, a second citation reversed in direction in the opening paragraph, a third cited for seasons it does not cover, an equation quoted at operating points where it has no real solution, and a cross-structure ratio whose endpoints match no computable quantity. A manuscript this careful with its own arithmetic should not be this loose with its references, and the contrast itself is worth the authors' attention: the checking process evidently covered computation and self-limitation but not attribution. The three changes that would raise the score most are, in order: repair the citation layer and bound the Theorem 5 increment explicitly against Held et al. (comments 1, 10, 13, 32); correct the two internally inconsistent numerical statements and state the applicability condition of Eq. (27) (comments 2, 3); and attach cluster-bootstrap intervals to every coverage rate with n reported alongside the pre-peak values (comment 5). All Major comments are repairable without new experiments. With those three done and the Figure 3 and Table 4 defects fixed, this would be a solid 7.

## 6. Limitations of this review

I did not re-run the authors' experiments, re-fit any model, or execute any code from the repository, and I did not compare any repository value against the PDF; conclusions about reproducibility are therefore about artefact presence and labelling, not about numerical agreement. What I did recompute independently, from the printed tables and closed forms, is listed above: the Theorem 5 recursion over 2,688 grid points, the Lemma 4 boundary limits, the Table 4 cross-structure ratios under every construction I could enumerate, the Eq. (27) radicand and its failure threshold, the Table 5 component shares, the Table 7 threshold sweep, the Table 9 width ratios, and Jeffreys intervals for the Table 10 coverage strata. Figure and layout findings were verified on 300 dpi re-renders, with 600 dpi for the p. 33 collision and true greyscale conversion for the colour checks, to exclude rendering artefacts. On references: I mechanically verified all 42 printed DOIs against the Crossref REST API and checked bibliographic records there and at Europe PMC. The paid academic search route was unavailable for the entire review — the upstream returned HTTP 401 on the single attempt, so zero paid queries succeeded — and all verification ran on the free route (Crossref, WebSearch, WebFetch, Europe PMC). I therefore obtained no full-text PDF for any load-bearing reference; the semantic claims in comments 7 and 13 rest on abstracts and publisher records and are marked as such, and the editor should ask the authors to verify them against the full texts. Seven candidate criticisms were tested and withdrawn after checking, including an initial claim that Figure 3 lacked a `\label` (it has one, at L614) and an initial claim of hard-coded equation numbers (zero occurrences); those are not part of this report.
