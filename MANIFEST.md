# Reproducibility Manifest

All numerical results in the manuscript are generated by a single build chain
(`scripts_v2/make_all.py`), which halts on any consistency failure.

Provenance of the processed inputs: state-level weekly hospital admissions
aggregated from the **terminal target data of the three CDC forecast hubs** —
COVID-19 (`reichlab/covid19-forecast-hub`, NHSN weekly hospital admissions; the
weekly panel is re-derivable from the daily truth file via
`scripts_v2/ingest_and_aggregate.py`), influenza (FluSight), and RSV (RSV
Forecast Hub). All three series are NHSN weekly hospital-admission indicators;
there is no cross-indicator splicing. The micro-layer files are the published
offspring data of Adam et al. (2020), Faye et al. (2015) and Lloyd-Smith et al.
(2005); the hub benchmark files are the official COVIDhub-ensemble archives
(12 forecast origins), checked against the upstream repository.

`ingest_and_aggregate.py` re-derives the COVID weekly panel from the daily file,
asserts structural invariants, and pins each deposited series by the national
window sums used in the manuscript (COVID-19 Delta 2021-07-03..2021-07-31 =
141,735; influenza 2022-10-08..2022-11-05 = 15,579; RSV 2024-11-09..2024-12-07 =
22,047), so a silent series swap fails the build.

## Build chain (`scripts_v2/`)

| Step | Script | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `ingest_and_aggregate.py` | data-integrity + identity assertions; checksum table above |

| 2 | `emit_v3.py` | `reports_v3/national_rolling.json`, `budget_national.json`, `tables_v3/*.tex` |

One command: `python scripts_v2/make_all.py` (add `--fast` to skip steps 5–7).

## Runtime (8-core desktop CPU, 16 GB RAM)

- steps 0–3 < 1 min; `verify_suite.py` ≈ 3 min; `sim_verify_t3.py` ≈ 1–2 min

## Consistency guarantees

`check_consistency.py` asserts: table fragments regenerate byte-identically from
the JSONs; every referenced figure exists and is non-blank; a stale-literal
blacklist is clean across `main.tex` and `README.md`; abstract headline ranges
match the JSONs; micro-layer bootstrap/CRB ratios match the fit JSON; every
error-budget drift row equals h_week × the per-week drift variance (weekly
clock); the rolling evaluation contains both persistence (up) and local-linear
(down) crossings; and the deposited-series identity pins hold.

## Legacy material

Pre-v2 scripts are kept unmodified under `scripts/legacy/` for the record; they
are not part of the build.
